# Complex Fluids Website

This is the website of our academic research group at the University of Manchester.

This website is powered by Jekyll and some Bootstrap, Bootwatch. 


The website is based on that for Allan Lab at the University of Leiden. We tried to make it simple yet adaptable, so that it is easy for you to use it as a template. Plese feel free to copy and modify for your own purposes.  You don't have to link to us or mention us (but of course we appreciate it).

Go to *aboutwebsite.md*  to learn how to copy and modidy this page for your purpose.
