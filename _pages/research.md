# Research

Our research sits at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics, complex and non-Newtonian flows, and data-driven modelling. We develop and apply advanced numerical methods to understand the rich behaviour of fluids that defy classical Newtonian assumptions — from polymer solutions and emulsions to reactive and multi-phase systems.
