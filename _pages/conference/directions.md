## Directions

The conference will be held in the Jocelyn Bell-Burnell lecture theatre in the [Schuster Building](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/about/maps/interactive-map/?id=52), home to the University of Manchester's Department of Physics and Astronomy. The Schuster Building is a 10-15 minute walk from the city centre and Manchester Picadilly station.

On the evening of the 15th December, the conference dinner will take place at [Manchester Museum](https://www.museum.manchester.ac.uk/), located on Oxford Road, a 5 minute walk from the conference venue.
